Milestone 5 · morph

Shot outcomes, as a donut and as columns

The prototype's third card with its morph, on the library: the season's shots as a donutChart and as columns on barChart, in one chartSwitch. Each slice turns into its own column and back with morph(), and its labels move with it.

Shot outcomes

Switch between share of total and side-by-side counts. The same four shapes morph into each other, so you never lose which slice became which column. Click again mid-way and they go back from where they are. With a slice or column focused, V switches too, and focus follows its outcome.

Morph

Two ways to morph

  • Drop is the lab's. Each slice's outer arc becomes its column's top, so the slice settles onto the baseline.
  • Unroll matches each side with the same side. The slice stands up: its outer arc becomes the column's left side, and the angle it spans becomes its height.
  • Either way, a switch back retraces the same path, from wherever the shapes are.

Differences from the lab's card

  • Each view is a whole chart, with its own legend, tooltip, keyboard order and data table, and only the one showing can be reached or read. The lab's card is one picture.
  • The button says "Show as columns", where the lab's says "Show as bars".
  • The old view's legend and table fade out by 40% of the way and the new view's fade in from half way, so for a moment neither shows.
  • V on a focused slice or column is new: it's how a keyboard user switches and keeps their place.
