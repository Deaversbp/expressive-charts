expressive-charts demos

  • Ball and cleat: the first card of the lab rebuilt on the real library, and the same story on a hand-written SVG chart through expressive() (milestone 1).
  • Shot outcomes, three ways: the lab's third card without its morph, on donutChart, barChart and a D3 pie through expressive(), with a hover effect picker and staggered flashes after each match (milestone 3).
  • Goals vs expected goals, two ways: the lab's second card on lineChart and on a D3 line through expressive(), with the goals line red below xG, a ball riding its leading edge, and one Play button and scrubber driving both (milestone 4).
  • Shot outcomes, as a donut and as columns: the lab's third card with its morph, a donutChart and category columns on barChart in one chartSwitch, each slice turning into its own column and back with morph(), and a control to compare its two mappings (milestone 5).
  • Stress cases: each case at sizes up to past its limit, with a frame readout: bars that update, hover on a large chart, years of daily values on a date axis played through playback(), D3 columns followed with watch, a switch with many pairs, and the first draw (milestone 6).
  • Expressive Chart Lab: the original single-file prototype (bars with ball and cleat hover, goals vs xG line, donut-to-bar morph).
