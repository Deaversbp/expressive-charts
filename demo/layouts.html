Layout morphs on D3 charts · hostView()

Bars, areas, a radar and a treemap that change their layout

Four cards, each a chart switch of charts drawn with D3 by code that knows nothing about expressive-charts. Each chart is attached with expressive() and made a view with hostView(), and morph() turns each mark into its own mark in the next layout, by its key. Nothing moves until you press a card's buttons, or V on a mark.

Goals and assists, three ways, on D3 bars

Each player's goals and assists side by side, stacked, or as shares of 100%. Each segment is a <rect> keyed by its series and player, so it slides from beside the other into the stack, then stretches to its share, and its number becomes a percentage. A jump from side by side to 100% goes through Stacked.

Shots, stacked and as shares, on a D3 area

Okafor against the league, from a D3 radar to bars

Six numbers per 90 minutes, each a percentile of the league's outfield players. Each value's <circle> grows into its bar, the player's above the league average's, and the outlines, rings and spokes fade. The axes' names move to the bars', and the percentiles fade in.

The season's scorers, from a D3 treemap to bars

The season's 58 goals by scorer, the tiles grouped by position and laid out by d3.treemap(). Each tile turns into its scorer's bar, most goals first, in its position's colour. A tile with room for its scorer's name and goals has them, and they move to the bar; a small tile's bar's fade in.

Design notes

  • Each view is a chart drawn by a D3 module that knows nothing about expressive-charts, attached with expressive() and made a view with hostView(). The switch pairs each mark with the mark that has its key in the next view, and morph() turns one into the other, so no effect knows the kind of chart it's on.
  • A bar's segments float in a stack, so they're given baseline: "left", and so are the treemap's tiles: each tile's sides then pair with its bar's. The radar's circles are read as points, and its areas' paths as shapes: two bands across the same weeks pair week by week.
  • A jump of two, from side by side to 100% or back, goes through Stacked in Presentation: two switches of about a second each, announced once, when it lands. In Analysis it goes straight there in a fifth of a second, and in Off at once.
  • Each label the page names moves with its mark and swaps its text half way: a count becomes a share, an axis's name a bar's. A label with no partner fades, as do the rings, the spokes and the axes.
  • Each card's views give a series or a position the same colour, through the page's CSS, so nothing changes colour as it morphs.
  • Focus follows its mark's key into the next view, and the switch announces the view it lands on. The buttons are toggle buttons and keep focus; V on a focused mark goes to the next view.

Things to try

  • Press 100% on the bars: each segment slides into the stack, then stretches, and its number becomes a share. Press Side by side to go back the same way.
  • Press 100% on the areas, and watch a week: its top and bottom move straight up or down.
  • Tab to a circle on the radar and press V: it grows into its bar, and focus stays on it there.
  • Pick Analysis, then press Bars on the treemap: the tiles become bars in a fifth of a second.
  • Press 100% on the bars, then Side by side part way through: the bars go back from where they are.
  • Turn on reduced motion and press any button: the new layout is there at once.
