Things to try

  • Hover a week on the line: its goals column and its shots column light up too. Then pick Emphasis only, and the linked weeks keep their emphasis and dimming without the ball or the pulse.
  • Tab into the goals per matchweek and use the arrow keys: the other two charts follow the focused week, and focus stays where it is. Escape ends all three.
  • Press Play next matchweek: a week is added to every chart, the players who scored flash with their change past their bar, the goals line's change rises past its end, and the slope chart's changes follow.
  • Pick Off, or turn on reduced motion: the changes and the slope chart's labels show at once, and a trace is drawn whole.
  • Open the players' data table: its Change and Since matchweek 1 columns say what the charts write over the bars and the lines.
