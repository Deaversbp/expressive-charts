Morph

Linked, largest first

The same shots on barChart, largest first, in one link() with the switch. Hover a bar, or Tab in and use the arrow keys, and its slice or column lights up in whichever view shows; hover a slice or a column, and its bar does. The link takes the switch's view on show, so it holds across a switch.

Two ways to morph

  • Drop turns each slice's outer arc into its column's top, so the slice settles onto the baseline.
  • Unroll, morph()'s default, matches each side with the same side. The slice stands up: its outer arc becomes the column's left side, and the angle it spans becomes its height.
  • Either way, a switch back retraces the same path, from wherever the shapes are.

Design notes

  • Each view is a whole chart, with its own legend, tooltip, keyboard order and data table, and only the one showing can be reached or read.
  • Each outcome keeps its colour in both views and in the bars beside them: the three charts share one colorAssignment, and the columns and bars colour by category.
  • The bars are in a different order from the slices and the columns, so the link goes by outcome, each mark's key, never by place. A linked mark shows no tooltip or focus ring, and nothing is announced: the chart under the pointer or the keyboard says it.
  • The old view's legend and table fade out by 40% of the way and the new view's fade in from half way, so for a moment neither shows.
  • V on a focused slice or column is how a keyboard user switches and keeps their place.

Goals and xG, with missing weeks

The line page's season as a weekly feed recorded it, with no values for matchweeks 9 and 10 and no xG for matchweeks 4 and 5, in a second chartSwitch. Goals and xG per match and season to date are each a lineChart, and goals per match are columns on barChart, keyed as the lines' points are (goals@12). Each point grows into its column, and the two charts' lines morph into each other with their gaps kept. With a week or a column focused, V goes to the next view, and focus follows the week. View

Points into columns

  • A line chart draws its points only when they're hovered, so each column grows out of its point's centre, from nothing, and shrinks back into it. You see which week's goals became which column. A crossfade would show none of that.
  • The lines have no partner among the columns, so they fade out over the first half of the switch, each with its end dot, and back in over the second half on the way back. xG has no columns, and its points aren't drawn, so only its line fades.
  • Each column is drawn in goals' colour all the way, as its point is: the three views share one colorAssignment, and the switch paints each mark as its own view does.
  • The end labels fade out, and the columns' axis and value labels fade in.
  • The columns leave out matchweeks 9 and 10: a column of no height would say no goals were scored. From either week on a line, focus goes to the first column.
  • Focus follows the week: from a week on a line to its goals' column, and from a column back to its week.

Line into line

  • From Per match to Season to date, each line morphs into the other week by week: morph() pairs two lines that run left to right by x, so each week's point moves straight up or down.
  • Gaps match by week too, so nothing is drawn in the weeks both lines miss, 9 and 10, and xG's 4 and 5, at any point of the switch.
  • Each line's corners are its own as the switch starts and lands, with morph()'s default points: the jagged line per match doesn't jump.
  • Each line's end dot moves with its end, with its ring, as the chart draws it.

Things to try

  • Switch in each mode: about 950 ms in Presentation, 200 ms in Analysis, and none in Off or under reduced motion.
  • Watch the labels: they move with their shapes and swap their text half way, while the centre total fades out and the axes fade in.
  • Switch with Drop, then with Unroll, and click again mid-way with each: the shapes go back the way they came.
  • Hover a bar beside the switch, then a slice: each lights up its partner. Switch to the columns and try again: the link follows the view on show.
  • Pick Columns below, then Per match: the goals points grow into columns and shrink back, while the lines fade. Pick Season to date and watch the gaps at matchweeks 4, 5, 9 and 10.
  • Tab into the line chart below, go to a week with the arrow keys, and press V three times: focus stays on that week through each view.
  • Go to a week, Shift+Tab back to the toggles and pick another view, then press Tab: you're on the same week, or its column.
