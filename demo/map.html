Where the squad comes from

A world map drawn with d3-geo by code that knows nothing about expressive-charts, with its effects attached through expressive(). Only the countries the squad comes from are marks; the rest is plain land. Nothing moves until you hover a mark or move to it with the keyboard, and the map never zooms or pans.

Harbor City FC's 25 players, by country, and the summer's signings

Each country is shaded by how many of the squad's players come from it. Hover one, or Tab to the map and use the arrow keys, which go through the countries by name: it's outlined and the others dim. Each line is one of the summer's four signings, from the city he came from to the club, the ring on England's north-west coast; an arrow travels it when you hover or focus it, and its country lights up with it.

Borders: Natural Earth's 1:110m countries, in the public domain, through world-atlas. They are de facto borders: Natural Earth draws each place by who controls it, so Kosovo, Northern Cyprus, Somaliland, Western Sahara and Taiwan are shapes of their own. The page takes no position on any of them.

Design notes

  • The map is drawn by a D3 module that knows nothing about expressive-charts: world-atlas's borders, read with topojson-client and drawn with geoPath() on geoEqualEarth(), an equal-area projection, so a country's size on the page is its size on the ground.
  • Every country is drawn as plain land, hidden from screen readers. The 13 the rows name are drawn again on top, a <path> each, and only those are attached: the land is context, and never reacts.
  • A country's path is read by its tag as a closed shape, keyed by its name. The module draws them in name order, since the arrow keys follow the document's order.
  • The outline on hover is a small effect of the page's own, made with defineEffect(): it draws the mark's outline in the overlay in the text colour, at once, and it's gone on leave. Nothing moves under the pointer, and dim fades the other countries in a tenth of a second.
  • Each route is a <path> from a LineString, which geoPath() draws as a great circle. It's read as an open shape, so travel("arrow") rides it. No route crosses the 180° meridian, where the projection would cut it in two.
  • The countries and the routes are two attachments, linked by country with link(): a route lights the country it comes from, and a country sends the arrow along its route.
  • The shades are three steps of the theme's first colour, in light and dark. Each country's count is in its name, the legend and the table, so nothing is told by shade alone.
  • With reduced motion or Off, the outline is the same, and the arrow rests at the club's end of its route.

Things to try

  • Hover Nigeria: it's outlined, the other countries dim, and the arrow travels Adeyemi's route to the club.
  • Hover the line from Japan: the arrow travels it, and Japan lights up.
  • Hover the sea, or a country that isn't shaded: nothing happens.
  • Tab to the map and press →: focus goes from Argentina to Czechia, then France, by name. Home and End go to the first and the last.
  • Tab again to reach the routes, and use the arrow keys to go through the four signings.
  • Make the window narrower: the map draws again at the new width, and the outline follows its country.
  • Pick Off, or turn on reduced motion, and hover a route: the arrow is at the club's end at once.
