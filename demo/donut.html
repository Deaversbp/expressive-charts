Shot outcomes, three ways

The prototype's third card without its morph: the season's shots on the library's donutChart and barChart, and on a pie drawn with D3. The same effects play on all three with no change to their code. Pick a hover effect, hover or Tab into a chart, then add the next match.

Shot outcomes

The library's own donutChart. The ball rolls round a slice, Wiggle rocks it a few pixels about the centre, and Pulse rings it twice. Next match flashes the slices whose counts changed, one after another (stagger(flash())). In Presentation a new order resizes the slices, then turns them into place.

The same story on a D3 pie

Drawn the way D3 code would draw it, with d3.pie() and d3.arc(). expressive() describes each slice with describe.arc and adds the same effects, dimming and keyboard focus, which goes clockwise from 12 o'clock though d3.pie() returns its arcs in data order. markLabel names the slices as the donut does, and refresh() follows each redraw.

Shot outcomes as columns

The same data and effects on barChart. The columns are one colour: a bar chart colours by series, and this one has a single series, shots. Matching each outcome's colour to the donut comes with the morph (milestone 5).

Milestone 3: done when

  • The donut is the lab's card 3 on the library: slices with separators, outside labels, the centre total, a legend, the tooltip, a data table with a Share column and a total row, keyboard order round the ring, and staged updates. Light and dark come from the library's defaults.
  • The same ball rolls round slices on the donut and on the D3 pie (expressive({ geometry })), with no change to travel or its gaits during the milestone.
  • Wiggle and Pulse work on the donut, the columns and the D3 pie. A wiggle moves no part of a mark more than 3 px, pulses end within 5 s, and in Analysis and Off the flashes after a match don't stagger.
  • checkEffect passes for every built-in effect on every fixture, and on real marks from barChart, donutChart, expressive() bars and D3 pies. The effect API isn't experimental, expressive-charts/testing is exported, and CHANGELOG.md states the 0.x promise.
  • Keyboard focus plays the stories: Tab into a chart, then the arrow keys, Home and End; Escape stops. Picking another effect keeps focus on the picker.
  • The donut screen-reader check in docs/a11y-check.md passes; the donut screenshots are green in CI, and the bar baselines haven't changed.
