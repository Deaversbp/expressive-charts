Goals vs expected goals, season to date

The library's own lineChart. The goals line turns red wherever the team has scored fewer goals than its chances were worth, and the ball rides its leading edge as the season plays. Hover anywhere, or Tab in and use the arrow keys, to read both lines and the gap week by week.

Differences from the lab's card

  • The ball riding the goals line is new; the lab has none. It's drawn at 11 px, smaller than its own 17, so it doesn't hide the last weeks.
  • xG is a dashed grey line, where the lab's is orange: orange sat too close to the red below xG, and the dash tells the lines apart whatever the viewer's colour vision. The legends draw each entry as a short line, so xG's shows its dash.
  • The goals line's end dot and hover dot keep their series colour, since threshold draws nothing on a dot. The tooltip's "vs expected" line and the table's Difference column still say when goals are below xG.
  • "Goals below xG" comes after the series in the legend, since a page's entries follow the chart's own.
  • The table's headers are the series' names, "Goals scored" and "Expected goals (xG)", and its values share the chart's one format, so an xG of 20.0 reads "20".
