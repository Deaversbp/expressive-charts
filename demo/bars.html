Milestone 1 · ball and cleat

Ball and cleat on the real library

The prototype's first card, rebuilt on barChart and travel, and the same story on a chart the library didn't draw. Hover a bar, then switch the motion mode, the metric and the sort.

Motion

Harbor City FC: goals by player

The library's own barChart. In Goals, a ball rolls the length of the bar. In Assists, a boot steps along it. In Goals + assists, the ball stops at the end of the goals and the boot carries on. Switching the metric or sort animates: in Presentation the values change first and then the rows move; in Analysis it's one quick step; Off jumps.

The same story on someone else's chart

A plain SVG chart drawn by hand, the way D3 code would draw it. It knows nothing about the library and redraws from scratch on every change, with no animation of its own. expressive() adds the same hover story, keyboard focus (Tab, then the arrow keys) and dimming, and refresh() keeps the effects in step after each redraw.

Milestone 1: done when

  • All three hover stories match the prototype, on both charts: travel speed and easing, the ball not clipped, the boot's sole down.
  • Sorting is staged in Presentation (values first, then positions), one quick step in Analysis, and instant in Off and under reduced motion (DevTools rendering emulation). Clicking again mid-change makes nothing jump.
  • Keyboard focus plays the same stories: Tab into a chart, then the arrow keys, Home and End; Escape stops.
  • Off and reduced motion: hovering shows the icons resting at their end positions.
  • The conformance tests pass for travel on bar marks from both sources (npm test).
