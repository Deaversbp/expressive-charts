Ball and cleat on the real library

The prototype's first card, rebuilt on barChart and travel. Hover a bar, then switch the motion mode and the metric to feel the difference.

Motion

Harbor City FC: goals by player

In Goals, a ball rolls the length of the bar. In Assists, a boot steps along it. In Goals + assists, the ball stops at the end of the goals and the boot carries on. Switching the metric or sort animates: in Presentation the values change first and then the rows move; in Analysis it's one quick step; Off jumps.

Feel checkpoint

  • Travel speed and easing in each mode, side by side with the prototype.
  • Icon size and placement: the ball isn't clipped, the boot's sole stays down.
  • Hover timing: the 100 ms colour change and the dimming of the other bars.
  • Off, and reduced motion (DevTools rendering emulation): the icons rest at their end positions.
  • Metric and sort changes: values first, then positions, in Presentation; one quick step in Analysis; a jump in Off. Click again mid-change: nothing jumps. A hovered bar replays its story once the change settles.
