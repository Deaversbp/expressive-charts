expressive-charts · examples

Three everyday charts

The library's own charts on business, research and web data. Every number is made up.

Business

Revenue by region

A quarter's revenue on barChart, each bar labelled with its share of its target by label(). Play the next quarter, and delta() labels each change: the values move first, then the rows.

Science

Seedling height under drought

Mean height by day on lineChart, for seedlings whose watering stopped on day 5 and for a watered control. threshold() colours the drought line wherever it's below the control. Hover a point, or Tab in and use the arrow keys.

Web analytics

Sessions by source

The last 30 days on donutChart, in a chartSwitch to bars: each slice turns into its own bar and back. With a slice or a bar focused, V switches too, and focus follows its source.
